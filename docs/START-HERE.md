# Dallas EMBA: start in three steps

Welcome! The [Dallas EMBA Cohort Hub](https://baylor-emba-proposals.martin-perrine-mp.workers.dev/) is an unofficial, student-maintained resource with Fall 2026 assignments, readings, quizzes, class meetings, and Class of 2028 program dates. It is not operated by Baylor. **Current Canvas requirements, syllabi and instructor instructions always win.**

## 1. Make your own private progress Sheet

Open [Dallas EMBA Progress Tracker — MAKE A COPY](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1PmvyTZAUdKsK27lK45FF8lXPSCMwpctZ-Cuy4_17bwc/copy). In Google's copy dialog, **rename your new file `EMBA Personal Progress` before choosing Make a copy**. This creates a new Sheet under YOUR Google account. The template includes 55 Fall assignment IDs and blank status cells. Keep your copy private. The public master is view-only; **do not** request edit access or put progress in the public Sheet.

## 2. Give your AI an EMBA personality

Download [EMBA Agent Instructions (.txt)](../cloudflare/public/downloads/EMBA-Agent-Instructions.txt). This is a **plain text file** used in setup, not an application to install. In Step 3, open it, copy the entire text, and paste it into the **Project instructions** field in ChatGPT or Claude, or the **Gem instructions** field in Gemini.

The assistant is set up to:
- check publicly available course deadlines, reading modules, class weekends and policy notes;
- keep your **Submitted** status separate from **Finished, not submitted**;
- read and (where your plan enables it) write your own private progress Google Sheet, verifying status plus timestamp;
- plan 7/14 days out and propose finishing 24–48 hours ahead of real due dates.

Follow your own platform's steps:
- [ChatGPT setup](SETUP-CHATGPT.md)
- [Claude setup](SETUP-CLAUDE.md)
- [Gemini setup (Gem + Gemini in Sheets)](SETUP-GEMINI.md)

**Paid AI plans are recommended for the most capable Google Sheets experience**, but they do not guarantee a direct write in a standard chat. [Eligible U.S. college students can claim Google's 12-month AI Pro student offer](https://gemini.google/us/students/) (verify eligibility, payment method required, redeem by Dec 31, 2026, renews paid unless cancelled). Gemini's custom assistant equivalent is a Gem; Google Sheets editing may occur in a separate panel.

## 3. Set up your AI, then ask what you need to do this week

After following your [ChatGPT](SETUP-CHATGPT.md), [Claude](SETUP-CLAUDE.md), or [Gemini](SETUP-GEMINI.md) guide and pasting the agent text in the appropriate **instructions field**, try the prompt below. The [10 common EMBA prompts](../cloudflare/public/downloads/EMBA-Common-Prompts.txt) are optional extras:

> "What is due or required before our next class weekend? Check the current shared EMBA Hub and my private progress Sheet. Include all known quizzes, readings and submissions and flag any provisional dates."

**Test one fictional Sheet edit** before assuming an AI can write on your Free or paid plan. If it can't, update your private Sheet manually and paste your statuses into your chat. Your AI must never claim it saved an update unless it can verify the write.

## Privacy and academic integrity
- **Public Hub:** shared academic requirements and event metadata only.
- **Your private Sheet:** personal completion statuses only; no grades or scores.
- **Canvas:** actual due dates, assignment text, instructor policies and real submission records.
- Never post your filled-in Sheet or private instructor materials in GitHub issues or the public Hub.

## Support or corrections
The Hub includes a human-reviewed correction process. Until no-login intake is security tested, submit a public-safe correction through the GitHub form or pass it to the cohort volunteer. Never attach restricted course text or individual student details.
