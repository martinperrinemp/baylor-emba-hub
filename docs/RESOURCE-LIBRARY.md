# Resources and lessons learned

`data/resources.json` lists useful links and tools, including Baylor templates and AI presentation utilities. Entries can be `candidate_needs_review` or verified. A public link does not guarantee all users can access it or that a template may be redistributed.

Before publication, check URL, ownership, access requirements, practical usefulness, privacy implications, and whether describing the resource could be mistaken for institutional endorsement. **Do not mirror proprietary slides, case texts or readings**.

`data/lessons-learned.json` accepts concise, generalizable, non-identifying accounts of workflows that did or did not help. They are experiences, not official policies. Don't submit classmates' assessed work or solutions.

**AI resource usage**: Checking a tool catalog is not enough. Assistants must also read `data/ai-policies.json` for course- or assignment-specific AI restrictions and disclose uncertainty when not verified. No general assumption of permission is allowed.
