Set up your EMBA assistant in Google Gemini

About 15 minutes. Do this on a laptop or desktop in Chrome.

You make one Sheet yourself. Google Gemini guides you through creating your private EMBA Planner Gem and connecting Google Workspace, one step at a time.

By the end you'll have

  • A private progress Sheet only you can see
  • A Gemini Gem called EMBA Planner that knows the cohort's rules
  • Google Workspace connected when available, with your private Sheet accessible for planning

What you need

  • A Google account with access to Gemini. Google AI Pro may enable additional Workspace features; basic custom Gems are also available on supported personal accounts.
  • A laptop or desktop web browser for creating and editing a Gem
  • Use the same Google account for Gemini and your private Sheet

No GitHub account, coding, or earlier AI experience is needed.

Why a paid plan? Weekly planning means long chats. Google AI Pro can provide access to additional Workspace features, including Gemini in Sheets for eligible accounts. You can still use supported free Gemini features for planning. Google plans to transition personal-account Gems to Skills starting November 2026; this guide will need to track that change. See Google Gemini plans and prices.

  1. Make your private Sheet (do this yourself)

    1. Open Make a copy of the class tracker.
    2. Rename it EMBA Personal Progress, then click Make a copy.
    3. In your copy, click Share and confirm access says Restricted.

    This keeps your personal progress separate from the publicly shared class template. Do not edit the class template.

  2. Copy the setup prompt

    This plain-text prompt tells Gemini how to walk you through creating an EMBA Planner Gem and connecting your Sheet, one step at a time. It is not software.

    Download the .txt file

    Show prompt

    This is the exact text used by the copy button. Scroll inside the preview to read it, or open the plain-text file.

  3. Paste it into a new Gemini chat

    1. Open Google Gemini and start a new chat. You don't need a Gem yet.
    2. Paste the prompt from Step 2 and send it.
    3. Gemini will walk you through setup. Reply done after an action, stuck if something looks different, back when returning from Google Drive, or attach a screenshot if you're unsure.

    Google Gemini should wait for your response before giving the next action. If it skips ahead, use the troubleshooting advice below.

Connection note: Google Gemini can use Google Workspace or a Drive file attached as Gem Knowledge to read your private Sheet where supported. Do not assume a Gem can write Sheet cells. For eligible accounts, edit through Ask Gemini inside Google Sheets on desktop or update the Sheet manually. Gem instructions · Gemini in Sheets.

Prefer to see every step in writing? Open the full written Google Gemini setup guide.

If something goes wrong

ProblemFix
Google Gemini skips ahead or loses trackType: “Please go back to step [number] and wait for my reply.”
Google Gemini can't open your SheetCheck Settings & help → Apps (or Extensions) → Google Workspace (some versions show Apps) and confirm you connected the Google account that owns your Sheet. Paste the link to your private copy again.
The setup chat gets too longStart a new chat, paste the setup prompt again, and say which steps you already completed.
You'd rather not use guided chatUse the full written guide.

Google Gemini chats/Gems and Gemini in Google Sheets have different capabilities. A chat read does not prove Sheet edit access. If available, use the Ask Gemini side panel in Sheets, review the action and click Apply, or update your private Sheet manually.

Privacy and good habits

  • Keep your Sheet Restricted. Record statuses only. Never add grades, points, personal names, or private Canvas links.
  • Never post your filled-in Sheet to the public Hub, GitHub repository, or correction form.
  • Review Google Gemini privacy and permissions before connecting Google. Use a private Project and don't share your filled tracker.
  • Canvas, syllabi, and instructors always come first. Before using AI on graded work, verify the assignment permits it. Unknown does not mean allowed.
