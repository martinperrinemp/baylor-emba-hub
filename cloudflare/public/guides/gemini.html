Gemini is currently unsupported

Our cohort's Gemini pilot could not reliably access current public assignment data or update a student's private Google Sheet. To avoid unreliable deadline and progress answers, we are pausing Gemini onboarding.

Use one of the working, tested setup guides instead:

  • Set up ChatGPT (guided)
  • Set up Claude (guided), including a tested Free account

If you used an earlier Gemini setup prompt, do not rely on it for current EMBA deadline or status tracking. We will reconsider support only after fresh access and status updates have been tested.
