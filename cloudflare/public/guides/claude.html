Set up your EMBA assistant in Claude

About 15 minutes. Do this on a laptop or desktop in Chrome.

You make one Sheet yourself. Claude guides you through everything else, one step at a time.

By the end you'll have

  • A private three-tab tracker with assignments and 104 Reading & Prep records
  • A Claude Project called EMBA Planner that knows the cohort's rules
  • Claude connected to your Sheet, with a test to verify whether updates really save

What you need

  • A Claude account. We recommend a paid plan (Pro or higher).
  • A Google account
  • Chrome on a laptop or desktop (or the Claude Desktop app)

No GitHub account, coding, or earlier AI experience is needed.

Why a paid plan? Weekly planning means long chats. Paid plans offer higher usage limits, making interruptions less likely. Free works for trying the planner, and you can update your Sheet manually if connected editing isn't available. See Claude plans and prices.

  1. Make your private Sheet (do this yourself)

    1. Open Make a copy of the class tracker.
    2. Rename it EMBA Personal Progress, then click Make a copy.
    3. In your copy, click Share and confirm access says Restricted.

    This keeps your progress separate from the class template. Do not edit the class template.

  2. Copy the setup prompt

    This plain-text prompt tells Claude how to walk you through setup, one step at a time. It is not software.

    Download the .txt file

    Show prompt

    This is the exact text used by the copy button. Scroll inside the preview to read it, or open the plain-text file.

  3. Paste it into a new Claude chat

    1. Open Claude and start a new chat. You don't need a Project yet.
    2. Paste the prompt from Step 2 and send it.
    3. Claude will walk you through setup. Reply done after a step, stuck if something looks different, or attach a screenshot if you're unsure.

    Claude should wait for your response before giving the next action. If it skips ahead, use the troubleshooting advice below.

Already have an EMBA Planner? Keep your existing private Project and Sheet. The updated public starter has a separate Reading & Prep tab, but older personal copies do not update automatically. Download the one-time reading upgrade instructions to add the tab safely.

Prefer to see every step in writing? Open the full written Claude setup guide.

If something goes wrong

ProblemFix
Claude skips ahead or loses trackType: “Please go back to step [number] and wait for my reply.”
Claude can't open your SheetCheck + → Connectors in the chat and make sure Google Drive and Google Sheets are on. Paste the link to your private copy again.
The setup chat gets too longStart a new chat, paste the setup prompt again, and say which steps you already completed.
You'd rather not use guided chatUse the full written guide.

Live Google Sheets editing is a beta feature and may not work for every account. Your Sheet remains the source of truth for confirmed progress. Claude can still make plans if you update the Sheet yourself.

Privacy and good habits

  • Keep your Sheet Restricted. Record statuses only. Never add grades, points, personal names, or private Canvas links.
  • Never post your filled-in Sheet to the public Hub, GitHub repository, or correction form.
  • On personal plans (Free, Pro, Max), chats may be used to improve Claude if you've allowed that. Check your privacy settings.
  • Canvas, syllabi, and instructors always come first. Before using AI on graded work, verify the assignment permits it. Unknown does not mean allowed.
