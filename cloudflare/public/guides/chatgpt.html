Set up your EMBA assistant in ChatGPT

About 15 minutes. Do this on a laptop or desktop in Chrome.

You make one Sheet yourself. ChatGPT guides you through everything else, one step at a time.

By the end you'll have

  • A private progress Sheet only you can see
  • A ChatGPT Project called EMBA Planner that knows the cohort's rules
  • Google Drive connected where available, with a test to check whether Sheet edits actually save

What you need

  • A ChatGPT account. A paid plan (Plus or Pro) is recommended for higher limits, but Free can work.
  • A Google account
  • A laptop or desktop web browser (easiest for setup)

No GitHub account, coding, or earlier AI experience is needed.

Why a paid plan? Weekly planning means long chats. Paid plans offer higher usage limits, making interruptions less likely. Free can work for planning, and you can update your Sheet manually if Google Drive actions aren't available. See ChatGPT plans and prices.

  1. Make your private Sheet (do this yourself)

    1. Open Make a copy of the class tracker.
    2. Rename it EMBA Personal Progress, then click Make a copy.
    3. In your copy, click Share and confirm access says Restricted.

    This keeps your progress separate from the class template. Do not edit the class template.

  2. Copy the setup prompt

    This plain-text prompt tells ChatGPT how to walk you through setup, one step at a time. It is not software.

    Download the .txt file

    Show prompt

    This is the exact text used by the copy button. Scroll inside the preview to read it, or open the plain-text file.

  3. Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat

    1. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat. You don't need a Project yet.
    2. Paste the prompt from Step 2 and send it.
    3. ChatGPT will walk you through setup. Reply done after an action, stuck if something looks different, back when returning from Google, or attach a screenshot if you're unsure.

    ChatGPT should wait for your response before giving the next action. If it skips ahead, use the troubleshooting advice below.

Connection note: ChatGPT uses the Google Drive app for supported Google Sheets actions. App availability and editing permissions vary by account. Learn more.

Prefer to see every step in writing? Open the full written ChatGPT setup guide.

If something goes wrong

ProblemFix
ChatGPT skips ahead or loses trackType: “Please go back to step [number] and wait for my reply.”
ChatGPT can't open your SheetCheck Settings → Plugins → Google Drive (some versions show Apps) and confirm you connected the Google account that owns your Sheet. Paste the link to your private copy again.
The setup chat gets too longStart a new chat, paste the setup prompt again, and say which steps you already completed.
You'd rather not use guided chatUse the full written guide.

ChatGPT reads and edits supported Sheets through the Google Drive app when available. A connection does not guarantee editing. Your private Sheet remains the source of truth for confirmed progress; you can update it manually if needed.

Privacy and good habits

  • Keep your Sheet Restricted. Record statuses only. Never add grades, points, personal names, or private Canvas links.
  • Never post your filled-in Sheet to the public Hub, GitHub repository, or correction form.
  • Review ChatGPT app privacy and permissions before connecting Google. Use a private Project and don't share your filled tracker.
  • Canvas, syllabi, and instructors always come first. Before using AI on graded work, verify the assignment permits it. Unknown does not mean allowed.
