GET STARTED / GEMINI

Full written Google Gemini setup guide

Prefer guided setup? Open the short Gemini setup page.

Gemini calls a reusable custom assistant a Gem, rather than a Project. Make the Gem for planning; spreadsheet editing may use Gemini inside Google Sheets.

Before you start: Have a Google account and your Gemini login. No GitHub account needed. This is easiest on a laptop or desktop browser.

  1. Make your private progress Sheet

    Open Make a copy of the class tracker. In Google's copy window, change the name to EMBA Personal Progress, then click Make a copy. Open your new file and check Share: access should be Restricted. You will see 55 assignment rows with blank Status cells.

  2. Copy the assistant instructions

    On the EMBA Hub, under Step 2, click Or copy full text. That copies the assistant's personality and rules. Prefer a file instead? Download the instruction .txt file, open it, and copy all the text. It is not software to install.

  3. Create a Gem and paste the instructions

    Open Gemini and sign in. Open the sidebar → Gems (sometimes Settings & help → Gems) → New Gem. Name it EMBA Planner. Paste all the text from Step 2 into Instructions, then click Save. Select your new Gem to start chatting. Under Knowledge → Add files → Add from Drive, you may attach only your private progress Sheet if supported. A Drive-backed knowledge file can reflect its latest saved content. Do not put the instructions only in a description. Google plans to transition Gems to Skills for personal accounts starting November 2026. If your interface differs, follow the current controls.

  4. Ask Gemini to find your progress Sheet

    In your EMBA Planner Gem, ask: “Find my Google Drive spreadsheet EMBA Personal Progress and read its Progress tab headings. Do not edit.” If Gemini offers to connect Google Workspace, review the sign-in permissions for the account that owns your Sheet. A Gem finding a Sheet does not mean it can edit it.

  5. Test editing inside Google Sheets

    On a computer, open your copied Sheet. Click Ask Gemini at the upper right (if your Google AI plan includes it). If you consent, ask for the fictional TEST-001 update below. Review the change and click Apply when prompted. Refresh the Sheet and check Status and Last Updated. If Ask Gemini is missing, your account may not have this Sheets feature. Ask permission before adding the fictional row, avoid duplicates, and only delete that test row after your own confirmation. Use only: Not started, In progress, Finished not submitted, Submitted, or Blocked.

  6. Plan from your saved Gem

    Return to EMBA Planner in Gemini and ask the weekly question below. If your Gem cannot directly read or write the updated Sheet, use the Sheets panel to edit and paste relevant status rows into the Gem chat. Gem chat and Gemini in Sheets are different tools; do not assume the two automatically share edit permissions.

Test: did it save?

Copy this into your new assistant (for Gemini's editing test, use the Sheets panel). Check the result in your own spreadsheet.

Only with my explicit approval, in MY private Google Sheet named EMBA Personal Progress, add one fictional row named "Test only" with ID TEST-001, then set Status to "In progress" and Last Updated to the current date and time. Read those two cells back and tell me if the edit really saved. Do not change any real assignments or the publicly shared class template.

You're ready. Try this next:

What do I need to complete before our next class weekend? Check the latest EMBA Hub deadlines and readings. Use my private Sheet for confirmed progress, and flag any unverified dates.

How Gemini finds dates

Read the public assignments.json, prep.json, manifest.json, and open-alerts.json files directly. Ignore record_status: superseded_aggregate and distinguish preparation dates from submission deadlines. Public Hub dates may be provisional. Your private Sheet has statuses, not deadlines. Canvas and instructors take precedence.

If you get stuck

A Google AI Pro subscription may unlock Gemini in Sheets but does not guarantee chat-to-Sheet editing. Eligible U.S. college students may qualify for a 12-month Google AI Pro offer; verify current terms and renewal details with Google. If editing fails, you can still use the planner and update your private Sheet yourself. Never trust “saved” without a read-back.
